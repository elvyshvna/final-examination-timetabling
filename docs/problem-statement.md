# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
• University scheduling administrators who spend excessive time manually managing complex schedules.
• Students who face unfair exam overlaps, poor spacing between tests, and increased academic stress.

## What exactly is going wrong?
• Manual planning methods fail to track conflicts across thousands of diverse student course enrollments.
• Exam clashes occur frequently, booking students for two different tests at the exact same hour.

## Why does it matter?
• It lowers academic performance because exhausted students cannot perform their best during back-to-back exams.
• It delays the university calendar, freezing operations when schedules must be completely redone due to errors.
• It spikes administrative overhead, wasting hundreds of hours of labor on fixing avoidable scheduling mistakes.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
University administrators struggle to organize final exam schedules manually for thousands of students. The current process frequently creates exam clashes, where students are scheduled for two tests at the exact same time. It also causes back-to-back exams that exhaust students and lead to unfair academic conditions.
